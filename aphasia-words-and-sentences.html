5.1 Auditory Word and Sentence Comprehension

Aphasia Home Practice · Interactive picture selection

Caregiver Instructions
Press the speaker button so the individual hears the word or sentence. Do not show the written target to the individual. Ask them to point to or tap the matching picture. Allow time to respond. Start with the easy level (two pictures), then medium (three pictures), then hard (four or six pictures). Word and sentence sets each follow these levels. Record the support provided after each response.
